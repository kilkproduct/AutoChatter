Important capability note

Message delivery is disabled in this build.

Valenced's public plugin API does not confirm an approved message-send interface or its response contract. AutoChatter keeps any overdue item pending and does not send—it does not call private Discord actions or raw HTTP.

What confirmation is needed? ↗

01 / BEHAVIOR

One clock.
One deliberate destination.

Every part of the scheduler is designed to avoid surprises: no automatic channel choice, no catch-up burst, and no background sends.

01 — GLOBAL COOLDOWN

One interval for everything.

The default is 7,200 seconds. Choose any whole-second interval from 600 upward. A confirmed send resets the single global clock; missed intervals never stack up.

DEFAULT2 hours
◎

02 — CHANNEL SAFETY

Only where you are.

No channels are selected initially. The current channel must be selected, accessible, and permission-checked at the point of a send attempt.

03 — FOREGROUND ONLY

Time to be present.

If an interval expires while inactive, one pending item waits for ten uninterrupted foreground minutes. Backgrounding restarts the grace period.

Aa

04 — YOUR MESSAGE

Plain text, exactly as written.

Start with the Meow preset or write your own. The editor validates against Discord's current composer limit when available, with a 2,000-character fallback.

02 / THE FLOW

A simple loop,
with careful checks.

  1. 01

    Choose your message & interval

    Nothing is sent when you edit settings.

  2. 02

    Select channels yourself

    Selection is explicit; the current channel is rechecked later.

  3. 03

    Wait for the one global deadline

    Offline time creates at most one pending send—never a backlog.

  4. 04

    Recheck activity & permission

    Ten continuous foreground minutes may be required after inactivity.

  5. 05

    Delivery stays fail-closed

    This build stops here until Valenced confirms an approved sender API.
